SCIENCE / SOURCES / ASSUMPTIONS

Understand the engineering.

This game teaches relationships between mission decisions. Its numerical specifications are educational assumptions, not measurements of real spacecraft.

Why choose radar during cloudy weather?

NASA explains that the NASA–ISRO NISAR mission uses radar to observe Earth's surface through clouds and in darkness.

In our game, cloudy turns reduce optical observation quality. Radar avoids that cloud penalty. The exact quality factors are fictional balancing values.

Read NASA's NISAR mission explanation
Why does collecting more data create problems?

NASA's small-spacecraft communications guidance describes trade-offs among data rate, power consumption, and mass.

In our game, observations fill limited storage. Sending them to Earth requires a contact window and transmitter power. Data still onboard does not count toward the delivery objective.

Read NASA's spacecraft communications guidance
How does the battery calculation work?

Power is measured in watts. Stored energy is measured in watt-hours. Each action lasts five simulated minutes.

Energy change = (generation - consumption) × 5 / 60

For example, a 60 W power deficit lasting five minutes consumes 5 Wh. Battery energy cannot exceed its capacity. An action that exhausts the battery ends the mission.

What is simplified or not yet modelled?
  • Hardware specifications, prices, launcher capacities, and science scores are fictional.
  • Contact windows, target passes, weather, and eclipses are scripted. No orbital trajectory is calculated.
  • Eclipse controls solar generation. Target illumination is not modelled separately, so an optical observation can still be offered during a spacecraft eclipse.
  • Battery charging is idealised: efficiency losses, temperature, ageing, and charging-rate limits are not modelled.
  • A one-hour operations exercise does not estimate a spacecraft's full service lifetime.
  • NASA publications support the educational explanations. The game does not download or analyse satellite imagery.
How does Second Chance make a fair comparison?

The game records your actions. You can change one instrument, solar array, or battery and replay them. Orbit, launcher, and scheduled external conditions remain unchanged.

If an action becomes unavailable, the replay substitutes conservation and reports the substitution. A battery failure can end the replay early.
